A cozy living room isn't about buying a new sofa. It comes from texture, warm light and a few personal pieces that make the space feel lived in. These twelve ideas work in rented apartments and small rooms, and most cost less than a dinner out.

## 1. Swap cool bulbs for warm ones

Lighting changes the mood of a room more than any piece of furniture. Look for bulbs labelled 2700K or "warm white", and use them in every lamp in the room so the light feels even.

<Tip>Turn off the main ceiling light in the evening and use two or three lamps at different heights instead.</Tip>

## 2. Add a second (or third) lamp

One overhead light makes a room feel flat. A floor lamp beside the sofa and a small table lamp on a shelf create pools of light that feel instantly calmer.

## 3. Layer a chunky knit throw

Draping a thick throw over the arm of the sofa adds texture you can see from across the room. Cream, oatmeal or sage green work with almost every sofa color.

## 4. Mix pillow textures, not just colors

Combine one smooth pillow, one textured (boucle, knit or tufted) and one with a simple pattern like a check or stripe. Keep to two or three colors so the mix looks intentional.

## 5. Put a rug under the front legs

A rug that's too small makes a room feel disjointed. Choose one big enough for at least the front legs of the sofa and chairs to rest on it; a jute rug is a durable, affordable base.

## 6. Bring in real (or very convincing) plants

Greenery makes a room feel alive. A trailing pothos on a shelf and one larger plant in a corner are enough; if you forget to water, good faux plants are fine.

## 7. Style a coffee table tray

A tray turns clutter into a small display. Group a candle, a small plant and a stack of two books, then keep the remote in a drawer.

## 8. Hang curtains high and wide

Mount the rod a few inches below the ceiling and past the window frame on each side. The window looks bigger, and floor-length linen curtains add softness instantly.

## 9. Add one handmade piece of wall art

A single meaningful print or textile above the sofa makes the room feel personal. Choose something slightly larger than you think you need: about two-thirds of the sofa's width.

## 10. Create a reading corner

An armchair, a lamp, a throw and a small side table make a cozy corner even in a small living room. It gives the room a second place to sit and relax.

## 11. Use baskets for hidden storage

Woven baskets hide blankets, toys and chargers while adding natural texture. Two matching baskets under a console table look tidy and intentional.

## 12. Finish with scent and sound

Coziness is more than looks. A candle or diffuser with a warm scent and soft background music make the room feel inviting the moment you walk in.

<Tip>Pick one "signature" scent for the living room and stick with it. It quickly starts to feel like home.</Tip>
