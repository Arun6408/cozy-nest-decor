Boho style is relaxed, layered and full of natural materials. In a bedroom it works especially well: warm colors and soft textures help the room feel calm at the end of the day. Here are ten ways to get the look without starting from scratch.

## 1. Start with an earthy color palette

Pick three base colors: a warm white or cream, a sandy beige and one earthy accent like terracotta, rust or olive. Repeating those three colors across bedding, pillows and art keeps a layered room from looking busy.

## 2. Layer linen bedding

Washed linen looks better slightly rumpled, which suits boho style perfectly. Mix a solid duvet cover with a contrasting sheet set and a textured blanket at the foot of the bed.

## 3. Add a rattan or cane headboard

A natural-fiber headboard is the fastest way to signal boho. If you can't replace the bed, a woven wall hanging or a large round mirror above it gives a similar effect.

## 4. Hang a macramé or woven wall piece

Woven textiles bring softness to a bare wall. Hang one centered above the bed, slightly wider than the pillows.

## 5. Fill the room with plants

Trailing plants on a shelf or a hanging planter by the window soften hard lines. A snake plant in a woven basket handles low light well.

<Tip>Group plants in odd numbers (three is perfect) at different heights for a natural, collected look.</Tip>

## 6. Use a patterned rug

A vintage-style rug in faded reds, rust and cream ties the palette together. Put it under the lower two-thirds of the bed so your feet land on it in the morning.

## 7. Choose warm, low lighting

Swap a harsh ceiling fixture for a rattan pendant shade, and add a small lamp on each nightstand. String lights along a shelf are a renter-friendly extra.

## 8. Mix wood and natural textures

Pair a wooden nightstand with a jute rug, a ceramic lamp and a woven basket. Varying textures matters more in boho style than matching furniture.

## 9. Display textured ceramics

A few textured vases or small dishes on the nightstand hold jewelry and add character. Matte, slightly uneven glazes look the most relaxed.

## 10. Add dried flowers and pampas grass

Dried arrangements last for months and bring warm, soft color to a dresser or windowsill. Keep them away from direct sunlight so the color lasts.
