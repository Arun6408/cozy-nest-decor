Renting doesn't mean living with bare walls and builder-grade everything. These twelve ideas transform a rental without drilling, painting or risking your deposit.

## 1. Try peel-and-stick wallpaper

A single accent wall behind the bed or sofa changes the whole room, and it peels off cleanly when you move.

## 2. Use adhesive hooks and strips

Removable hooks and picture-hanging strips hold art, mirrors and coats without a single hole.

<ShopGrid>
<Product store="amazon" name="Picture hanging strips" query="picture hanging strips removable" note="Follow the weight rating and press firmly for 30 seconds." />

## 3. Lean art instead of hanging it

Lean framed prints on a dresser, mantel or picture ledge. Layering two or three frames looks relaxed and intentional.

## 4. Add plug-in wall sconces

Plug-in sconces add cozy lighting beside a bed or sofa with no electrician. Cord covers make them look built in.

<ShopGrid>
<Product store="amazon" name="Plug-in wall sconces" query="plug in wall sconce set of 2" note="Instant ambient lighting without touching the wiring." />
</ShopGrid>

## 5. Swap cabinet hardware (and keep the originals)

New knobs and pulls make a dated kitchen or dresser look fresh. Store the originals in a labelled bag to put back when you move out.

## 6. Cover ugly floors with rugs

Large rugs hide worn carpet or dated tiles and add warmth instantly.

## 7. Use a tension rod for curtains

Tension rods fit inside window frames with no drilling. Great for small windows and closets.

<ShopGrid>
<Product store="amazon" name="Tension curtain rod" query="tension curtain rod black" note="Expands to fit inside the window frame." />
</ShopGrid>

## 8. Replace the shower curtain and bath mat

A textured shower curtain and a thick bath mat transform a bland rental bathroom for little money.

## 9. Add a freestanding shelf or bookcase

Freestanding storage gives you display space without mounting anything to the wall.

## 10. Bring in plants

Plants make any room feel more alive and personal, and they move with you.

## 11. Use a large leaning mirror

A tall mirror bounces light and makes small rooms feel bigger, no hanging required.

## 12. Change light bulbs to warm white

The cheapest upgrade of all: warm bulbs make every room feel cozier.

<Tip>Take photos of every wall and fixture on move-in day. It makes the move-out inspection much easier.</Tip>
