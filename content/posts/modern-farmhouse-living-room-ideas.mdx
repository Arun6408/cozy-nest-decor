Modern farmhouse mixes rustic warmth with clean, simple lines. Think warm whites, natural wood, a few black accents and plenty of soft textiles. Here's how to bring that look into your living room.

## 1. Start with warm white walls

A creamy white with a hint of warmth is the backdrop of most modern farmhouse rooms. Avoid stark, blue-toned whites, which feel cold next to wood.

## 2. Mix wood tones

Pair a lighter oak coffee table with a darker side table or picture frames. Two or three wood tones look layered and natural.

## 4. Layer neutral textiles

Combine a linen sofa slipcover, a chunky knit throw and pillows in cream, oatmeal and soft grey. Texture does the work that color does in other styles.

## 5. Hang a statement mirror or clock

A large round mirror or an oversized wall clock above the sofa or mantel is a classic farmhouse focal point.

## 6. Bring in greenery in simple vessels

Eucalyptus stems in a stoneware jug or olive branches in a glass vase add life without fuss.

## 7. Use baskets everywhere

Wire, seagrass and wood baskets hold blankets, toys and magazines while adding to the cozy look.

## 8. Add a vintage-style rug

A faded, vintage-look rug in muted blues, greys or rust keeps a neutral room from feeling flat.

## 9. Style the coffee table simply

A wooden tray, a candle, a small plant and two books. Less is more with modern farmhouse.

## 10. Choose warm lighting

Lamps with linen shades and warm bulbs keep the space inviting at night. Add a dimmer if you can.
