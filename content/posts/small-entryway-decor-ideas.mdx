Your entryway is the first thing you and your guests see, and in small homes it's often just a patch of wall by the door. These ten ideas make it practical and welcoming without taking much space.

## 1. Add a slim console or shelf

A narrow console table or wall shelf gives keys, mail and sunglasses a landing spot.

## 2. Hang a mirror above it

A mirror makes a small entry feel bigger and gives you a last look before leaving.

## 3. Use hooks instead of a coat rack

Wall hooks take up less space than a freestanding rack and keep coats off the floor.

## 4. Get a slim shoe cabinet

Shoes are the biggest entryway clutter. A slim tipping shoe cabinet hides several pairs in a few inches of depth.

## 5. Add a small bench

A bench gives you somewhere to sit and put on shoes. Pick one with a shelf or baskets underneath.

## 6. Keep a catch-all tray or bowl

A pretty tray or bowl for keys stops them disappearing every morning.

## 7. Lay a washable runner

A washable runner protects floors, catches dirt and adds pattern and color.

<ShopGrid>
<Product store="amazon" name="Washable entryway runner" query="washable runner rug entryway" note="Throw it in the washing machine after muddy weeks." />

## 8. Use baskets for each person

One basket per person for hats, gloves and small items keeps everything sorted.

## 9. Add a plant or vase

A small plant or a vase of dried stems makes the space feel welcoming.

## 10. Light it well

A small lamp or a plug-in sconce makes coming home in the dark feel warm and safe.

<Tip>Keep only what you use daily in the entryway. Seasonal coats and extra shoes go to a closet.</Tip>
