Winter is the best excuse to make your bedroom a true retreat. A few changes to bedding, lighting and textures make the room feel warmer the moment you walk in. Here are eleven easy ideas.

## 1. Switch to flannel or brushed cotton sheets

Flannel and brushed cotton feel warm to the touch on cold nights. Choose a soft neutral or a subtle plaid.

A quilt or coverlet folded at the foot of the bed adds warmth and makes the bed look plush and inviting.

## 3. Add a faux fur or sherpa throw

A fluffy throw on the bed or chair is the most wintry texture you can add.

## 4. Put a soft rug where your feet land

Stepping onto a cold floor first thing is the worst. A plush runner on either side of the bed fixes it.

## 5. Swap harsh overhead light for lamps

Use bedside lamps with warm bulbs in the evening. A dimmable lamp lets you wind down slowly.

## 6. Hang heavier curtains

Thicker curtains block drafts and early dark mornings. Lined linen or velvet panels add warmth and texture.

## 7. Add more pillows (but not too many)

Two sleeping pillows, two shams and one accent pillow is enough to look plush without a nightly pillow pile-up.

## 8. Bring in winter greenery

A small potted evergreen, eucalyptus stems or a pine sprig on the nightstand adds a seasonal touch that lasts all winter.

## 9. Use a diffuser for a calming scent

A warm, woody or lavender scent in the evening makes the room feel like a retreat.

## 10. Create a reading spot

A chair, a throw and a small lamp in the corner give you somewhere cozy to sit that isn't the bed.

## 11. Keep surfaces clear

A calm bedroom has little visual clutter. Keep only a lamp, a book and one decorative piece on each nightstand.

<Tip>Make your bed every morning. In winter especially, a made bed with a throw on top makes the whole room feel warmer.</Tip>
