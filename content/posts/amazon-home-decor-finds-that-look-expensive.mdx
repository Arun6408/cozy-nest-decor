## How to make them look even pricier

1. Group items in odd numbers and vary their heights.
2. Repeat materials (wood, brass, linen) in at least three spots in the room.
3. Leave some empty space on every shelf and surface.
4. Hide cords and tidy cables; nothing cheapens a room faster.
